JAMCORD - your own Discord music bot
====================================

FIRST TIME (about 5 minutes)

1. Double-click Jamcord.exe.

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
   then "Run anyway". This shows up because the app isn't from a
   big company, not because something is wrong.

2. A black window opens and your browser opens the Discord
   Developer Portal. In the browser:
     - Log in and click "New Application". Give it any name.
     - Click "Bot" in the menu on the left.
     - Click "Reset Token", confirm, then click "Copy".

3. Go back to the black window, paste the token
   (right-click or Ctrl+V) and press Enter.

4. Your browser opens again. Pick your Discord server and
   click "Authorize". The bot is now in your server.

5. In Discord: join a voice channel and type
       /play never gonna give you up


EVERY TIME AFTER THAT

Double-click Jamcord.exe. That's it.
Keep the black window open while you listen. Closing it stops the bot.


GOOD TO KNOW

- Keep this whole folder together. Jamcord.exe needs the files next to it.
- Your token is saved in a file called ".env" in this folder.
  Never share it. Anyone with the token controls your bot.
- To add the bot to another server, look for the line
  "Invite me to another server: ..." in the black window
  and open that link.
- If the window says the token didn't work, start Jamcord again
  and paste a new token (step 2).
- If music suddenly stops working, download the newest
  Jamcord version. YouTube changes things now and then.
